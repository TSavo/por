# Bane treasury retaliation

The saved party starts one square south of the treasury at (5,6), facing north.
Search and move Up, Open the trapdoor, then press Enter on the XP notice.
Choose Exit and No to leave the treasure. The original script starts
the retaliation if the half-orc cleric is still alive.

The checkpoint was resaved through Encamp in the bundled 1.3 runtime.
The original 1.3 and bundled traces have the same fixed-clock schedule.
At aaff, PIC1 block 4 member 0 draws once at ab06. Two idle keyboard
polls after the battle notice reach the battlefield and the orc's turn.
The replay uses no extra keys or developer combat.
