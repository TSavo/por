# Kobold throne ballista battle entry

This controlled snapshot places the party at the royal chamber route after the first throne wave. The existing local save uses a high-health party to let the ballista volley finish. Up opens the volley, and the two damage acknowledgement prompts are read with Enter. The Chrome replay checks the display switch at the damage acknowledgement. It then observes two browser-driven polls from the battle notice to the opening wild-boar combat frame.
