# Tempus City Watch battle notice

Slot A comes from the recorded Guide party in [guide-party-new-phlan/A.json](../guide-party-new-phlan/A.json). The controlled copy places that party at (0,13) facing north and sets ECL global `4ac0` to 1, the condition set when the party attacks the City Watch. The party and its equipment are unchanged.

Press Up into the temple. The City Watch challenge leads directly to the battle notice. The picture clears when combat starts.
