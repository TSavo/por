AS YOU ENTER THE CAVE YOU ARE GREETED BY DIOGENES, THE SILVER DRAGON. HE SAYS 'I SEE YOU HAVE RECOVERED THE SILVER BOTTLE FROM THE KOBOLD CAVES. I CONGRADULATE YOU.' 'THAT BOTTLE CONTAINS A POWERFUL EFREET WHO WAS SUMMONED TO KILL VAMPIRES. HE WILL BE A VALUABLE ALLY IN OLD PHLAN. IN VALHINGEN GRAVEYARD THERE IS A VAMPIRE.' 'WHEN YOU MEET THE VAMPIRE USE THE BOTTLE AND YOU MAY HAVE A CHANCE IN DEFEATING HIM. I WISH YOU LUCK.' AFTER SPEAKING DIOGENES FLIES THROUGH A HOLE IN THE CEILING AND DISSAPPEARS.
