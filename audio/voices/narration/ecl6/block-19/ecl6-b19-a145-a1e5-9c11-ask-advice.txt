THE CHARACTERS HEAR A LOUD FLAPPING NOISE AND THEN A WARM RUSH OF AIR HITS THEIR FACES. STANDING BEFORE YOU NOW IS A GREAT SILVER DRAGON. HE SAYS, 'YOU ARE WISE TO SEEK MY COUNCIL AND I CAN HELP YOU...' 'FAR TO THE EAST THERE IS A LARGE KOBOLD COMPLEX. THERE IS A SILVER BOTTLE OF GREAT POWER THERE THAT WILL HELP YOU ON YOUR QUEST. RETRIEVE THAT BOTTLE THEN RETURN AND I WILL SEND YOU ON ANOTHER QUEST.' AFTER SPEAKING, THE DRAGON FLYS THROUGH AN OPENNING IN THE CEILING OF THE CAVE. YOU EXIT THE CAVE TO CONTINUE YOUR QUEST.
