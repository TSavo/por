YOU ARE JUST OUTSIDE A PEN FOR HOLDING SLAVES. THE PEOPLE HERE ARE WORN AND BEATEN, DESTINED TO A LIFE OF SLAVERY. AROUND THE PEN THERE ARE MANY GUARDS AND SLEAZY MERCHANTS LOOKING AT THE SLAVES. YOU SEE A BOY THAT HAS A HEART-SHAPED BIRTHMARK ON HIS LEFT SHOULDER. AS YOU STARE AT THE BOY, A SLEAZY FAT MERCHANT APPROACHES YOU AND SAYS ... 'THAT YOUNG BOY WILL BRING A GOOD PRICE AT ZHENTIL KEEP. I THINK I SHALL BUY HIM.'
