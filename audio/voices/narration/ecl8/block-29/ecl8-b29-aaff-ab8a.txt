A WIDE-EYED WOMAN IS SEATED ON A RUG. AT YOUR ENTRY, SHE STANDS. 'GREETINGS BOLD ONES, I HAVE LONG AWAITED YOUR COMING. MY TIME HERE IS SHORT FOR THE WORLD ENTERS A NEW AGE. AN EVIL SPIRIT FROM AN UNHOLY POOL GUIDES YOUR ENEMIES. IT HIDES BEHIND A FAIR COUNTENANCE. BE NOT DECEIVED.' WITH THAT, THE WOMAN IS GONE.
