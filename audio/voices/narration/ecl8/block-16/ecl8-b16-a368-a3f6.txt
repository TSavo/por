SEEING THEIR LEADER FALL, THE OTHER LIZARD MEN WITHDRAW. THE OLD LIZARD MAN TURNS TO YOU, 'YOU HAVE DONE ME GREAT SERVICE. REST ASSURED THAT MY PEOPLE WILL NOT JOIN TYRANTHRAXUS.' 'STILL MY PEOPLE STILL ARE HOSTILE TO STRANGERS. I THINK IT IS BEST IF YOU LEAVE.' HE TURNS AND DISAPPEARS INTO THE WILDERNESS.
