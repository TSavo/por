FERRAN SPEAKS: 'THE CITY FELL LONG AGO TO THE UNBLESSED CREATURES IMBUED WITH THE MIGHT OF A MAGICAL POOL. CHIEF AMONG THESE WERE TYRANTHRAXUS, EDRANKA, AND TORATH. WITH THEIR POWERS THEY RULED AND UNITED ALL ELSE, DRIVING FORWARD TO DESTROY US ALL. THE SAGE MENDOR WORKED HARD TO GATHER RECORD OF ALL THESE THINGS, BUT THEY ARE LOST NOW, HIS LIBRARY OVERRUN.
