THIS IS THE DESK OF YARASH. ON IT IS A LITTER OF PAPERS. AMONG THE PAPERS ON THE DESK YOU FIND THE FOLLOWING AND SLIP THEM INTO YOUR JOURNAL: A LETTER FROM THE BOSS, TELLING YARASH TO JOIN OR DIE. (ENTRY 33) AN UNSENT REPLY TO THIS LETTER. (ENTRY 49) A MESSAGE TO THE BUCCANEERS OFFERING A BOUNTY FOR A REAL SAHUAGIN. (ENTRY 27) A WARNING TO AN AGENT ABOUT THE DANGERS IN THE MOUNTAINS TO THE NORTHWEST. (ENTRY 56) A NOTE TO A TRAPPER ABOUT KOBOLDS AND HOBGOBLINS. (ENTRY 40)
